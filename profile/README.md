## What is Ascendance Open Worlds?

**Ascendance Open Worlds** develops and preserves open-source technology for interactive 3D environments.

Our work connects classic virtual-world technology with modern development: **VRML97 and X3D runtimes, world-building tools, 3D content pipelines, interoperability utilities, archival work, and experiments in persistent online spaces.**

We believe old virtual worlds are worth preserving, open standards still matter, and useful 3D tools should remain understandable, portable, and available to the people who build with them.

> [!NOTE]
> **Ascendance3D** is the GitHub home for Ascendance Open Worlds. Projects will move here deliberately as they become part of the broader open-worlds toolset.

## Current work

### FreeWRL modernization

The active [FreeWRL fork](https://github.com/DJAscendance/freewrl) is modernizing the long-running open-source VRML97/X3D browser for current systems, including native Apple Silicon macOS work, automated regression testing, standalone packaging, and preservation of legacy 3D content.

FreeWRL remains an independent open-source project with its original authors and upstream history. Ascendance Open Worlds contributes modernization and preservation work around that codebase.

### WRL Forge

[WRL Forge](https://wrlforge.com) is a cross-platform desktop tool for editing, previewing, inspecting, and packaging classic VRML97 `.wrl` content.

It combines a native VRML97 editor with live [X_ITE](https://create3000.github.io/x_ite/) preview, gzip-transparent file handling, Cybertown item inspection, world-project scanning, and deterministic project packaging. Public builds are available for Linux, Windows, and Apple Silicon macOS.

- **Website & downloads:** [wrlforge.com](https://wrlforge.com)
- **Source:** [DJAscendance/wrlforge](https://github.com/DJAscendance/wrlforge)

WRL Forge is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cybertown or its current or former operators.

### Cyber Town Next-Gen

[Cyber Town Next-Gen](https://github.com/Cyber-Town-Next-Gen) focuses specifically on restoring, documenting, and extending Cybertown-era software and worlds.

Cybertown-specific projects remain under their own organization. Ascendance Open Worlds provides the broader home for reusable 3D, VRML/X3D, world-building, and preservation technology.

## Contributing

Public projects will include their own build, test, contribution, and licensing instructions.

For engineering work, we prefer:

- focused changes with clear scope;
- reproducible builds and tests;
- evidence before compatibility claims;
- preservation of project history and attribution;
- documentation that explains both **what** changed and **why**.
